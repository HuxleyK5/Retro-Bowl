How to play

Your first game

Move the ball to the right. Reach the far end of the field for a touchdown, worth 6 points. Your defense plays automatically when the other team has the ball.

  1. Choose a play. Start with a short pass or a run. Press Space to start the play.
  2. Throw or run. To pass, hold the mouse on the field, pull back opposite the direction you want to throw, then release. After a catch or handoff, your runner moves forward automatically. Use WASD or arrow keys to steer.
  3. Reach the yellow line. You get four tries to gain 10 yards. Reach the yellow line to earn four new tries. On your last try, you can also kick for 3 points or kick the ball far away to give the other team a longer trip.

A down means a try. A quarter is one of four parts of the game. A field goal is a kick worth 3 points; an extra point is a kick worth 1 after a touchdown. Press Escape to pause whenever you need a break.

Opening this guide during a game pauses play. Close the guide and press Resume when you are ready.
